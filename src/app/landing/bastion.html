    01 · The money engine

    Every action moves a balance. Instantly.

    Whenever money moves, Bastion does two things at once: it writes a permanent ledger line and adjusts the balance of the account it touched. They always happen together, so your books reconcile themselves.

    • Two money pools: every bank account and one company cash box
    • A double-entry journal writes itself under every payment
    • Un-disburse a salary and every effect reverses, exactly
    • Double-payment is blocked: a payslip is claimed before money moves

    • Relievers get per-day, per-client attribution
    • Leave beyond allowance docks pay automatically

    Core capabilities

    The plumbing of a security company, handled.

    Six things every guard-deployment business does the hard way. Bastion does each of them once, properly, and then keeps doing it.

    1. 01
      Attendance & Payroll

      Attendance, entered once — payroll done automatically.

      The daily board runs client-shift by client-shift and presumes everyone present, so your office enters only the exceptions and confirms the shift. Payroll then computes itself for every guard: prorated by the days worked, leave applied against the contract allowance, advances and deductions netted off. No more month-end Excel marathons.

    2. 02
      Profitability

      Know exactly which clients make you money.

      Bastion tracks what you bill against what you pay, and shows client-by-client profit with head-office costs properly accounted for.

    3. 03
      Invoicing & Accounting

      Your invoicing and books, built in.

      Set each client's invoice structure once and generate a whole month of invoices in one click, exported to PDF. Full accounting — ledgers, receivables, client statements and a complete bank trail — all inside Bastion.

    4. 04
      Guard Lifecycle

      Every guard, fully managed.

      From hiring and CNIC / police verification to documents, kit issuance and final settlement — the entire guard lifecycle in one system, with kit cleared before anyone leaves.

    5. 05
      Compliance

      Never miss a renewal again.

      Licences, NOCs and contract renewals tracked automatically, with email alerts well before anything expires.

    6. 06
      Partners & Regions

      Everyone's share, settled from the same books.

      Profit split by your own participation rules, a running statement per partner, and regional P&L with head-office cost allocated out — all read from the same ledger that paid the guards.
