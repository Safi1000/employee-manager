    01 · The money engine

    Every action moves a balance. Instantly.

    Whenever money moves, Bastion does two things at once: it writes a permanent ledger line and adjusts the balance of the account it touched. They always happen together, so your books reconcile themselves.

    • Two money pools: every bank account and one company cash box
    • A double-entry journal writes itself under every payment
    • Un-disburse a salary and every effect reverses, exactly
    • Double-payment is blocked: a payslip is claimed before money moves

    02 · Attendance becomes money

    A tick on Tuesday is a rupee next month.

    Mark who showed up. Bastion prorates each salary by attendance, pays leave within the contract allowance, docks leave beyond it, nets off any outstanding advance, then builds one payslip per guard, ready to pay by cash, bank or cheque.

    • Present / absent / leave, half-days, late and overtime
    • Relievers get per-day, per-client attribution
    • Leave beyond allowance docks pay automatically
    • Outstanding advances recovered without manual maths
